Composting rule, exactly as in the model: a household composts only when its awareness reaches the full tier (E ≥ 0.8) and it owns a composter. Convergence can move the whole population across a threshold at once, which is why the composting count can jump up or collapse to near zero even though nobody's equipment changed. Everything is the awareness layer only; waste volumes and capacity are omitted.
